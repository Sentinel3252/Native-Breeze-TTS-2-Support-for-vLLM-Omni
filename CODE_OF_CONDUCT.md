# Community guidelines

These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces maintained by the project.

- Treat participants respectfully regardless of experience or background.
- Discuss technical choices with evidence; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Welcome questions, accept corrections, and credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private recordings, personal information,
  or credentials without permission.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and repeated personal attacks
  are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive
discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate
and protect the privacy of people involved.

Use the hosting platform's private reporting tools for abusive behavior. For
a project policy question, open an issue without identifying private parties
or repeating sensitive content. Maintainers should provide a private contact
route in the repository before inviting reports that need confidential handling.
